Neya Companion Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Neya Companion is the iPhone and iPad companion for Neya on macOS. It lets you connect to your paired Mac, continue a conversation, view task status, send requests, and stop active work. This policy explains what information the companion handles and how it is used.

Information Neya Companion handles

Pairing and connection information. The app stores the information needed to authenticate your paired Mac and establish the connection. Pairing secrets are stored in the device Keychain. The app may use your configured private network connection, including Tailscale, to reach your paired Mac.

Messages and workspace status. Messages you submit, replies, task status, and other information you choose to view are exchanged between the companion and your paired Mac. Neya does not operate a central Neya account database for this companion release. The paired Mac may retain conversation history and workspace data according to its local settings.

Provider data. When your Mac is configured to use Ollama Cloud, the Mac may send the prompt, selected conversation context, approved memory, and approved tool results to Ollama Cloud to generate a response. Ollama handles that information under its own policies. Neya Companion does not collect or store your Ollama password or API key.

Optional diagnostics. This release does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Apple may process information required for App Store, TestFlight, crash reporting, and device services under Apple’s policies.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your paired devices, deliver messages and responses, show current workspace status, carry out actions you explicitly request, stop active work, and diagnose connection failures when you choose to provide diagnostic information. Neya does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

When information is shared

Information is shared with:

  • your paired Mac, to provide the companion features;
  • Ollama Cloud, only when your Mac uses Ollama Cloud for an authorized request; and
  • network or platform providers needed to deliver the connection or the app, such as Tailscale and Apple, subject to their policies.

Neya does not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Retention and deletion

Neya does not maintain a separate cloud account or central conversation store for this companion release. Data may remain on your paired Mac or in the app’s secure local storage until you remove it. You can revoke the pairing on the Mac, remove local app data, and delete conversation or workspace data using the controls provided by Neya. Some Keychain data may remain on the device until it is explicitly removed by the app or device settings.

Security

Neya uses authenticated device pairing and stores pairing material in the platform Keychain. No security method can guarantee absolute protection, so keep your devices and Apple account secure and pair only with a Mac you trust.

Children

Neya is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Neya changes. The date above shows when this page was last revised.
